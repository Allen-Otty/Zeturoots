KECOBO Licensing & Platform Guidelines

Welcome to ZetuRoots. By accessing or using our streaming services, mobile PWA, or payment gateways, you agree to comply with these terms.

1. Copyright & Intellectual Property Protection

All sound recordings, musical compositions, artwork, and metadata available on ZetuRoots are protected under the Kenya Copyright Board (KECOBO) regulations and international copyright treaties. Users may not rip, redistribute, or use tracks for uncredited commercial broadcasting without verified licensing.

2. Safaricom Lipa Na M-Pesa Payments

Event ticket purchases, VIP subscriptions, and artist tips are processed through authorized Safaricom Daraja APIs. Digital tickets with cryptographic QR passes are non-refundable once admitted at event venue gates.

3. Artist & Creator Revenue Share

Registered creators receive transparent royalties calculated from stream counts, completion rates, and in-play sponsor revenues, disbursed directly to registered M-Pesa accounts in full compliance with Kenyan tax regulations.

Data Privacy Policy

Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) Compliant

ZetuRoots is strictly committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

1. Data We Collect

We collect your registered name, Safaricom phone number, streaming preferences, and regional county selection to deliver localized music experiences and process ticket purchases.

2. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive information, including authentication sessions and payment verification hashes, is encrypted in transit and stored in secure cloud environments adhering to ISO/IEC 27001 security standards.

3. User Rights

Under ODPC statutes, you have the right to request access to your data, update your details, or request permanent deletion of your account and streaming history at any time.
